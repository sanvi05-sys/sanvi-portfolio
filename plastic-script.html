"Plastic is Everywhere" - YouTube Short Script

A script on Plastic Awareness, wrote for Biodegradable plastics brand.

Text with voice-over

“Plastic is everywhere in the world around you…”

Visual
  • A busy Indian snack/tea stall on a bustling street with:
  • Hanging bags of chips
  • Plastic water bottles stacked
  • Food packets dangling in the shop
  • Disposable plastic cups
  • Plastic carry bags given to customers
Text with voice-over

“…but have you ever wondered how much of it ends up inside us too?”

Visual

Camera smoothly zooms in to a man standing near the stall, he’s surrounded by all these everyday plastics.

Text with voice-over

“They’re in the water you drink…”

Visual

Close-up of the man unscrewing a plastic bottle, taking a sip of water.

Text with voice-over

“…the food you eat…”

Visual

Close-up of the man holding a packet of chips.

Text with voice-over

“…the clothes you wear…” [optional]

Visual

Macro close-up of shirt fabric or collar, showing fibers.

Text

“…the toys your kids play with…”

Visual

Plastic toys in the hands of kids playing nearby.

Text with voice-over

“…and the air you breathe.”

Visual

Street view with a car or auto rickshaw driving past, exhaust visible in the air and the pollutants smoke coming out of factory or industry behind.

Text with voice-over

“Studies say that on average, almost every human unknowingly consumes about 120–200 milligrams of plastic every day.”

Visual

(stat reveal)

Text with voice-over

“Plastic not just harms the planet, but also your health, from metabolism to cancer, its impacts are real and deathly.”

A better future begins with your choice for our kids and for Mother Earth
